Houston, March 16, 2026 – Vibrantz Technologies today announced that Scott Becker has been named President, Color Solutions, effective March 16, 2026. He succeeds Barry Misquitta, who has departed the organization after 10 years. In his new role, Becker will oversee the global strategy and day-to-day operations of the Color Solutions business unit.
