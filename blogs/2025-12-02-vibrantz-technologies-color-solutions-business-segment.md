HOUSTON, Dec. 2, 2025 – The Color Solutions business unit within Vibrantz Technologies recently announced its 2026 Color of the Year. The team’s color science and commercial experts have chosen “Infinity Blue,” a deep and calming blue that evokes stillness, clarity and a sense of grounded reflection.
